# Candidate knowledge boundaries

Reviewed September 21, 2026. These are general consumer guidance sources, not evidence that any one user's sleep score has a particular cause.

- [NHLBI: Insomnia treatment](https://www.nhlbi.nih.gov/health/insomnia/treatment): supports general sleep-habit and relaxation options and professional CBT-I treatment for longer-term insomnia. The candidate uses those broad categories without turning CBT-I sleep restriction into an unsupervised experiment.
- [NHS: Insomnia](https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/insomnia/): describes varied contributors including stress, shift work and health conditions, and practical sleep-habit guidance. The candidate avoids deriving diagnoses from that list or prescribing fixed clock times independent of a user's schedule.

The candidate removes the baseline's automatic mappings from HRV/stress to vagus activation, night waking to mineral problems, and brain fog to inflammation. These narrow causal claims are not established by the supplied user data or the above general sources. This is a proposed instruction change, not a measured treatment benefit.

Do not add supplement doses, treatment changes, mechanism guarantees or triathlon training prescriptions without separate evidence and review. Sources are explicitly pinned in this document; no runtime web retrieval or new provider is introduced.
