Get the beta on TestFlight

Open this link on your iPhone. Setup takes about five minutes.

HOW TO JOIN

  1. Install TestFlight from the App Store. It is Apple's free app for trying apps that are still being tested.
  2. Tap the invite link on your iPhone, then tap Accept and Install.
  3. Open the app, sign up, and answer a few questions about your sleep. You will get your first thing to try tonight.

INSIDE THE APP

✦Coach

Your sleep data, a short morning check-in, and one small change to try tonight. Talk with your coach about stress, travel, or what makes your routine easier or harder.

↗Progress

See your recent sleep scores and check-ins alongside what your coach is noticing. Patterns build over time; a score change alone doesn’t prove an experiment helped.

○Settings

Wearable data is optional. Connect Apple Health or an Oura ring to fill in your score, or log it yourself each morning. Set your reminder time here too.

GOOD TO KNOW

This is an early build, so expect a few rough edges. When something breaks, take a screenshot and tap Share in TestFlight, or email logan@30daysleepcoach.com.

Apple does not share its own Sleep Score with apps, so the app works out a score from your Apple Health sleep data instead. It will not match the number in the Health app.
